# User stories — Smart Campus study room booking

### US-01
**Story:** As a Student, I want to view which study rooms are free and when, so that I can choose a suitable room and time before booking.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** Availability reflects existing bookings and whether a room is blocked.

### US-02
**Story:** As a Student, I want to book a free room for a specific time slot, so that I have a reserved space to study.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** A booking lasting exactly two hours is permitted.

### US-03
**Story:** As a Student, I want to cancel a booking I made, so that I free up the room for others when I no longer need it.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** A student can cancel only their own bookings.

### US-04
**Story:** As a Student, I want to receive confirmation when my booking or cancellation succeeds, so that I know the requested action was completed.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** Confirmation refers to the outcome of the booking or cancellation action, rather than a reminder about a future booking.

### US-05
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to change a room's blocked status, so that I can control whether the room is available for booking.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** A blocked room cannot receive new bookings until it is unblocked.

### US-06
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to review room usage over a chosen period, so that I can understand how the library's study rooms are utilized.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** Usage is assessed from booking information, without tracking actual attendance.
